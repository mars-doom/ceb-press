# Build it yourself

The kit: one used ~25-ton log splitter, ~80 lb of plate welded into a mold box, and
dirt. If your soil passes the gates below, you get bricks. The vault stacks
form-free — each course leans on the party wall — and the earthwork is a winch and
a slip pan.

### Tools Required

Welder (one mold-box day — borrow or contract it), angle grinder, drill, shovels,
wheelbarrow, marked scoop, kitchen sieve / hardware cloth, mason jars (soil tests),
tarp, string + stakes, level, mallet.

### Other Prep Notes

- ~9–11 days solo all-in: ~3–4 pressing + ~2.5 stacking + ~5–6 earthwork
- Have an anchor plan for the winch (deadman or vehicle) before scrape days
- Inspect any used splitter before buying: tonnage rating, cylinder drift/leak,
  hose condition, pump noise, tire/bearing state for the tow home (≤55 mph — flatbed
  or trailer for long hauls)

### Step 1: Buy the splitter

Any towable ≥20T splitter works — size bricks to YOUR tonnage:
face psi = tons × 2000 ÷ brick face in², target 500–1,500 psi
(run `files/brick_calc.py press`). 25T → 8×12″ jumbo ≈ 520 psi.

### Step 2: Weld the mold box

3/8–1/2″ plate bore ~⅛″ oversize on the largest product; ~1″ platen on a stub shaft;
dead-stop collar for height; bottom-hinge eject door; top fill gate. Mounts on an
end-shoe cradle through-bolted below the beam flange — force bears on the beam end,
never on clamps. Add the in-line gauge tee.

### Step 3: Gate the soil (decides everything)

- **Jar test** for clay fraction — CEB wants roughly 10–30%
- **Squeeze-ball**: holds together at ~4–6% native moisture, cracks on drop. Mist
  liters/ton only if under 4%
- **24-hr soak/efflorescence** on a test brick — salinity gate
- **Crush on the press** — gauge at failure gives MPa; a failed native soil is a
  site finding, never a purchase

!!! warning "Watch out"
    If the soil fails the gates, the fix is a different site or a different method —
    not buying cement. That constraint is the whole point.

### Step 4: Press

dose (marked scoop = volumetric) → close gate → extend to dead-stop → dwell ~3 s →
retract → shoe out → push-through eject → rack to air-cure. ~1–2 bricks/min manual.

### Step 5: Stack

Plinth keys first (below-grade starter courses), battered rib walls, party wall with
its corbelled door void, then the lean vault — each band leans ~15° onto the wall.
Standard bricks on curves/rings, jumbos on wythes and liner.

### Step 6: Bank and cap

Winch-anchored slip scraper drags spoil into the counterforts and haunch banks in
compacted lifts; crest anchor pulls the slip up the face for the top lifts and the
~10″ armored cap. Pavers armor the crests.

!!! warning "Watch out"
    The loose-fill floor (~0.76g screening) means the keys and lift compaction are
    load-bearing details, not cosmetics. Tamp every lift.

### Step 7: Close up

Rear gable, vent stack + shutters, storm threshold, party-wall door leaf. Amenities
(second flue + stove, paver floor, bed platform) follow the same pressed-brick logic.
